Not sure what you mean by ‘in sync’, but have you tried the poll feature?  
It’s in the advanced settings (gear icon) on the right side of the PME editor.

  * Click the gear icon, then go to the poll menu (3 horizontal lines) just below the gear icon.
  * There you can select more filtering options.
  * In your case adding ‘armature object’ to your pose mode pie may stop it from interfering with weight paint mode.
