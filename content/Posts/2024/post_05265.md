Hi - I’m testing Pie Menu Editor on Blender 4.3b.

My thoughts:

  * Manually copying the PME 1.18.5 folder from the previous Blender version (in my case 3.6.13) is the only method that works for me.
  * When trying to install version 1.18.7, some of my hotkeys stopped working.
  * I also noticed that the Hair Tool addon conflicts with PME (I turned it off and deleted it from the addons folder; the previous version in Blender 3.6.13 seems to work fine), so be aware of potential issues with other addons.

I need to spend more time with the debug window to provide more insights. After any successful steps, make backups of your settings.

Good luck with your migrations, everyone!
