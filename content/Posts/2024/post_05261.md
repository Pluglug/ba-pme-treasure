PME didn’t keep me from moving to the latest version of blender. I use it every day, I’m on 4.2.3 LTS. Give it a try and see if something you use regularly doesn’t work. It’s super easy to test since you can back up blender’s addon folders by just moving or renaming them. Of course, when testing make sure you’ve exported a copy of your menus so you can always import them back. Fixes for things that didn’t work (for me, anyway) I found in this thread.
