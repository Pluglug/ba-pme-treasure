Ultimately, I’m of the opinion we should decide for ourselves here since it is clearly abandonware, whether intentional or otherwise. Wouldn’t you rather get the tool into more hands?

Your effort is incredibly appreciated here, but situations like this should be handled appropriately. If you are wanting to maintain PME without source control, this tool will either fade into obscurity or you’ll end up putting in constant extra effort to get it in the hands of the existing user base. There are a multitude of other reasons why having a centralized codebase is preferable, I’d be happy to divulge.
