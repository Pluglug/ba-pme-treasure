> Hi, I have a toolbar and I would like to add it to a new layout.  
>  When I click on pie menu editor, I don’t have te possibility to select it.

Rename Popup Dialog to **_Toolbar Top_**. In this case the popup dialog will be displayed in all layouts.  
Or you can create another popup dialog and name it **_Toolbar <Layout Name> Top_**.
