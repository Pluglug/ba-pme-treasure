One option is to make a pie menu per mode. When you make a pie menu, click the button next to the arrow in the picture below and choose your mode to have the pie menu show up in.

![](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/b/d/9/bd9f3a7a0ca2f4e05e10adbb84ee4c42f37758c9.png)  

For example, switch it from “Window” To “Object Mode.” Now the menu only shows in Object Mode. Hope that helps, if even a little.
