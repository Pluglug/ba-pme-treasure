Btw, PME has [input_box](<https://archive.blender.org/wiki/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Scripting/#Command_Tab_Functions>) function which can be useful here:

<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/9/7/2/97260c4bbe1ffd922d34b58998381f081f2771ec.mp4>
    
    
    bpy.ops.mesh.primitive_cube_add(); input_box(prop="C.object.name")
    

Or:
    
    
    bpy.ops.mesh.primitive_cube_add(); input_box(func=lambda value: setattr(C.object, "name", value)))
