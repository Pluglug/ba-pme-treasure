> How would I make a button to toggle between 2 workspaces?

Add a `Stack Key` with 2+ slots. Eg 2 slots (`Command` tab):
    
    
    C.window.workspace = D.workspaces['Default']
    C.window.workspace = D.workspaces['Scripting']
