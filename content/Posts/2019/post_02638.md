> i only want X lock rotation toggle  
>  how to achieve this?

[L.prop()](<https://docs.blender.org/api/blender2.8/bpy.types.UILayout.html#bpy.types.UILayout.prop>) function has `index` argument. You can use it in `Custom` tab:
    
    
    L.prop(C.object, "lock_rotation", text="", index=0)
