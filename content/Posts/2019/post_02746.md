> I have **Invalid Syntax** error

You are using quotes inside quotes: `'bpy.ops.wm.tool_set_by_id(name='builtin.select')'`

Replace inner quotes with double quotes: `'bpy.ops.wm.tool_set_by_id(name="builtin.select")'`
