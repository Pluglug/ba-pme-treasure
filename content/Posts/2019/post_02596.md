### What’s New

  * Option to [Remember StackKey State](<https://i.imgur.com/fi7T4I2.png>)
  * Bug Fixes

### How to Update

  * Backup your pie menus using _Export_ button.
  * Open Preferences.
  * Go to the _Add-Ons_ tab.
  * Click _Install_ button, navigate to the file you downloaded and install it.
  * **Restart Blender**.
