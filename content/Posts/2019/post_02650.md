What about the keymap? Where you added this `Context Toggle` entry?  
Looks like number keys are reserved in some of 3D area keymaps. `Alt+Shift+4` works in `Object Mode`, `Mesh`, and other 3d view object editing keymaps.

Also you have an error in `Context Attributes` field. It should look like this:
    
    
    preferences.inputs.use_rotate_around_active
