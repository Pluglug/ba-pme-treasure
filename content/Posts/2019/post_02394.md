> Can all functions be realized via the costum tab?

Not all but most of them.

![](https://blenderartists.org/letter_avatar_proxy/v4/letter/c/977dab/48.png) cyso:

> Tried the following:  
>  L.operator(bpy.ops.mesh.primitive_monkey_add(), text=“”, icon=‘MESH_MONKEY’)

Try this:
    
    
    L.operator("mesh.primitive_monkey_add", text="", icon='MESH_MONKEY')
    

Your second command should also work but in this case you need to manually add [execution context and undo flag](<https://docs.blender.org/api/blender_python_api_current/bpy.ops.html#keywords-and-positional-arguments>) (to be able to adjust operator settings):
    
    
    L.operator("pme.exec", text="", icon='MESH_MONKEY').cmd = "bpy.ops.mesh.primitive_monkey_add('EXEC_DEFAULT', True)"
