> Addon is great but I have problem. I wanted to make something similar like on this video:  
>  <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JdbmDSV9wIU>
> 
> So I made Pie menu and added Pop-up dialog which have Modifiers panel, but everytime I want to change something in modifiers using my new Pie menu, it turns off every time I click or change something, it doesn’t work like on video above.  
>  Here is example <http://i.imgur.com/xI799v6.jpg>

This behavior cannot be changed for pie menus, unfortunately.  
If you want to keep Modifiers panel open you need to assign a hotkey to Pop-up dialog and use it in [Panel mode](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fhI2imoo4U&feature=youtu.be&t=38>).
