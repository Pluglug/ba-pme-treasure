Mighty Pea:

> Upgrading the addon broke my context sensitive menu, and I figured out it was because the context_sensitive_pie script was now gone.

Did you press _Remove_ button in addon’s preferences (to remove the addon) before update? Only that button can remove the script, afaik.  
To update the add-on you just need to use _Install From File_ button in user preferences and restart Blender.

Mighty Pea:

> Alternatively, should I now set up context-sensitive scripts another way?

You can store your scripts outside of _pie_menu_editor/scripts_ folder and use absolute paths in pie menus:
    
    
    execute_script("absolute path to script")
