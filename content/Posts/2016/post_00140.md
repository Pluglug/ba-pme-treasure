![](https://blenderartists.org/user_avatar/blenderartists.org/chafouin/48/629120_2.png) chafouin:

> Is there a way to make the pie menu close as soon as you release the shortcut, like in maya?

I don’t know how it works in maya but by default in blender you need to:

  * press and hold the key to open the pie
  * move the mouse to select an item
  * release the key to confirm the selection and close the pie

![](https://blenderartists.org/user_avatar/blenderartists.org/chafouin/48/629120_2.png) chafouin:

> And is it possible to chain up nested pies in one flick of the mouse? Right now I have to stop and do the move again for the second pie,

Afaik, it’s impossible in blender. Even if you use confirm threshold option to make a selection in nested pies in one flick, you have to stop moving the mouse in each pie to confirm the selection.

Try to experiment with threshold and confirm threshold settings to minimize the delay. (for example: threshold=0, confirm threshold=5)
