> Adding Context Particles in a popup dialog button it will crash Blender with segfault when trying to add a particle system.  
>  Using Blender from buildbot, ubuntu.

Unfortunately, you cannot use panels with lists in popups. It’s a known blender’s [issue](<https://developer.blender.org/T47657>).

**UPDATE:** the issue was **fixed** and the fix will be available in the next blender’s version.
