“Y” hotkey opens 2 different dialogs - **Object: MESH** and **Object: LAMP.** You can add PMs for [other object types](<https://www.blender.org/api/blender_python_api_2_77_1/bpy.types.Object.html#bpy.types.Object.type>) (eg **Object: ARMATURE** for rigs)
