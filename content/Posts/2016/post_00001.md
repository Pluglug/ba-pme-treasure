## 💬 Content

[Pie Menu Editor](<https://gum.co/pie_menu_editor>) is an add-on for **Blender 2.79+/3.0+** that allows to create [pie menus](<http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=COW109EjBsU&index=2&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>), [regular menus](<http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvDADkqMLEw&index=3&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>) and [popup dialogs](<http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JdbmDSV9wIU&index=4&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>) without coding.

#### Features:

  * [Pie Menu Editor](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=COW109EjBsU&index=2&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>)
  * [Regular Menu Editor](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvDADkqMLEw&index=3&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>)
  * [Pop-up Dialog Editor](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JdbmDSV9wIU&index=4&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>)
  * [Macro Operator Editor](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x4HhN4aHCxg>)
  * [Modal Operator Editor](<https://archive.blender.org/wiki/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Editors/Modal_Operator>)
  * [Property Editor](<https://archive.blender.org/wiki/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Editors/Property>)
  * [Side Panel Editor](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BGs0de6ZYdw&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg&index=16>)
  * [Toolbar Editor](<https://archive.blender.org/wiki/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Toolbars>)
  * [Hiding panels](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z19c6b7XOWo&list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg&index=17>)
  * [Extending panels](<https://archive.blender.org/wiki/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Panels#Extending_Panels>)
  * [Sticky Keys](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pcFOPWnHC7U>)
  * [Stack Keys](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4wZKiyKE_P4>)
  * [And more…](<https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLsowJ3v5QWhE9db_GcPnSrTXWJrA5poWg>)
