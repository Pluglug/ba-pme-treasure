> If I want to use it on a normal pie, how do you use that ?

Try this code:
    
    
    pie.prop(bpy.context.object.modifiers["Solidify"], "thickness", "")
    
    

Or this:
    
    
    pie.box().prop(bpy.context.object.modifiers["Solidify"], "thickness", "")
