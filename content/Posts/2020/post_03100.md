> Are they saved in the blend file? Or get lost after restart of Blender?

Yes, they will be lost after restart.  
If you want to save data in blend file add a new `Property` item and press `Store in ...` button in [advanced settings](<https://archive.blender.org/wiki/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Editors/Property/#Storing_Values>). Select `Scene` for example. To access the data use this code:
    
    
    C.scene.MyProperty
    C.scene.MyProperty = "My Value"
