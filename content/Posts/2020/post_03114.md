> It is possible to make space beetwin menus wilder?

Open `scripts/addons/pie_menu_editor/operators.py` file and modify 2 lines:
    
    
    gap.scale_y = 5
    

Will add an option in add-on preferences in the next version.

![](https://blenderartists.org/user_avatar/blenderartists.org/pinhead/48/528486_2.png) Pinhead:

> FYI. in console i have lots warning:  
>  “Warning: Pie menus with more than 8 items are currently unsupported”

Yes, 10 slots are still officially not supported in Blender.  
You can hide these messages using some system tools.
