> Why do I get a PIE at the editor but I get a Menu in the windows?

You are using the same RMB hotkey for the pie and add-on’s context menu: `PME Preferences -> Settings tab -> Hotkeys tab -> PME Hotkey`.  
Try to replace it with something else. Eg. default hotkey is _Ctrl+Shift+`_
