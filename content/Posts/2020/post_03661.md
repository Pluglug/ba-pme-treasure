You should be able to follow this

  1. ADD **Panel Group**  
Customize your panel name
  2. Open ‘Interactive Panels’
  3. Select the item you want to add to this menu.
  4. Click on **Add as Panel to Panel Group**.  
That should be the effect you’re looking for.

您应该可以按照这样做

  1. ADD **Panel Group**
  2. 自定义您的 面板名称  
3.打开 ‘Interactive Panels’  
4.选择您想要添加到这里菜单的项目  
5.点击 **Add as Panel to Panel Group**  
这样,应该就是您想要的效果了
