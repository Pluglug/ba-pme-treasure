![](https://blenderartists.org/letter_avatar_proxy/v4/letter/s/7993a0/48.png) sleeplessmind:

> 1, what is the correct category for curve operations such as duplicate curve point and move.

Try `Curve` keymap.

![](https://blenderartists.org/letter_avatar_proxy/v4/letter/s/7993a0/48.png) sleeplessmind:

> so any chance PME can somehow add the view3d.manipulator function in 2.8 industrial compatible?

No, not in PME.  
Go to `Edit - Preferences - Keymap`, filter hotkeys by name `gizmo tweak` and enable `Any` Modifier for `Generic Gizmo - Gizmo Tweak` hotkey.
