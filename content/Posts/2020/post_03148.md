Hi Metin… I meant “realtime” as in recording a sequence of actions into a file and let PME play that sequence back when you put it on a button… Sort of keyframes in an animation sequence…

The trick to learn PME ( from my pov ) is to open the Info page in Blender and check what every action is giving you… copy and paste that code into buttons and whatnot…

EDIT: Maybe you need to turn on Debug mode in PME… but don’t quote me on that.
