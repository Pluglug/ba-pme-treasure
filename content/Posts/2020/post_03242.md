[@PhoxyShmoxy](</u/phoxyshmoxy>) pointed you in the right direction. You need to:

  1. make a separate menu (type: Popup dialog),
  2. assign functions to the menu’s entries,
  3. then assign the menu as an entry to a regular pie menu, with these steps:

Press the little icon with the lines at the right of the pie menu entry that should contain the submenu, and choose Edit Item. To avoid confusion: in the screenshot the ‘Bump and Displacement’ menu is already assigned to the pie menu slot, but usually the slot is still empty.  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/a/4/7/a47d5abd048fe8f9175cd3bd215b28a8f7672bdd_2_690x260.png)image857×324 47.5 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/a/4/7/a47d5abd048fe8f9175cd3bd215b28a8f7672bdd.png> "image")

Press the Menu button and choose the submenu you’ve created from the list:  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/8/8/e/88edd8c2b50c2fa26bc53d455446c08c36fe739f_2_690x217.png)image1500×473 52.7 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/8/8/e/88edd8c2b50c2fa26bc53d455446c08c36fe739f.png> "image")

Activate the Expand Popup Dialog option, then press OK to confirm:  
![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/f/1/f/f1f1b37aff96a400cde34ed5924e3292d522ba1d.png)
