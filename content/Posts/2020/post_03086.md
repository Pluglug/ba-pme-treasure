It’s because LMB is confirming the extrude as soon as it happens. Try disabling “Release Confirms”.  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/6/4/f/64feb324aafdaa404b8269e8feb464e1d111c747_2_690x254.png)image698×257 33.2 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/6/4/f/64feb324aafdaa404b8269e8feb464e1d111c747.png> "image")

  
Or if you’re using one of those extra buttons on the mouse, try mapping it to a regular keyboard key, like "" for example. You can map it either in your mouse driver or use something like AutoHotkey.
