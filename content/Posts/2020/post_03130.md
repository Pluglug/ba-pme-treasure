Yes… If you click “Settings” button in PME, to the right of the Settings button there’s a small icon that says “Interactive menus”( the text will pop-up if you hoover the mouse over the icon)… If you click on that icon there will pop-up red “buttons” all over Blender that you can click on and get a drop-down that gives you the option to " Extend menu items" …

…Then you go into PME and edit the functionality of the button…
