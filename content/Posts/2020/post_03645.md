### How to Update:

  * Backup your pie menus using `Export` button (optional).
  * Open `Edit > Preferences`.
  * Go to the `Add-Ons` tab.
  * Click `Install...` button, navigate to the file you downloaded and install it.
  * **Restart Blender.**
