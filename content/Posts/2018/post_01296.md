> Hi everyone, [@roaoao](</u/roaoao>), just an idea, why not using the center of a pie menu as a button too? I think it could bring more advantages than disadvantages, don´t you think???

I don’t think it’s a good idea. Center is used to cancel/close accidentally called pie.  
To simulate center button you can set [Tweak mode](<https://wiki.blender.org/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor#Hotkey_Mode>) for the pie. And add a Stack Key with the same hotkey, same keymap and [Press mode](<https://wiki.blender.org/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor#Hotkey_Mode>). Here is an example ([1 Hotkey - 3 Pie Menus](<https://wiki.blender.org/index.php/User:Raa/Addons/Pie_Menu_Editor/Examples#1_Hotkey_-_3_Pie_Menus>)).
