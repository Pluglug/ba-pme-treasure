> How can i map brush strength on hotkeys? Example + = 0.1, - = -0.1

You can [use stack keys to assign hotkeys to the code](<https://i.imgur.com/9BsnH8X.mp4>) (increase/decrease brush strength):
    
    
    brush(ups().use_unified_strength).strength += 0.1
    
    
    
    brush(ups().use_unified_strength).strength -= 0.1
