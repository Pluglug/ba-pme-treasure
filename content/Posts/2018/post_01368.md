## 💬 Content

For anyone using MESHmachine and PME - calling MESHmachine operators from a custom menu made with PME - and experiencing the issue of operators ignoring MESHmachine userpref options/ behaviors (modal ON/ OFF), change the “Execution Context” of the item from “Invoke (Default)” to “Exec”.  
Invoke = modal always ON  
Exec = modal always OFF
