![](https://blenderartists.org/letter_avatar_proxy/v4/letter/c/9fc29f/48.png) cet77:

> Is it possible to have another pie menu open on mouse over? I’d like to have a main-super pie menu that contains all other add-on’s menus and pie menus…

Yes, use [Confirm Threshold](<https://i.imgur.com/h2g9yCQ.gifv>) to open sub-pies on mouse over. Note that you need to hold main pie’s hotkey down to open sub-pie.
