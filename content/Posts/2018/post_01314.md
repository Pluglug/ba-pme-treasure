> I’d love for the cursor not to snap to the middle of the screen, but I’ll take what I can get!

Added a [script](<https://i.imgur.com/oV5lASt.mp4>) for this in PME 1.14.14:  
_edge_ \- the edge you want to move (‘TOP’, ‘BOTTOM’, ‘LEFT’ or ‘RIGHT’)  
_delta_ \- direction and distance in pixels  
_move_cursor_ \- move the mouse or restore the position
