In those cases the interactive panel option can help you.

Right click any button in the ui and click Pie Menu Editor, then the interactive panel option

![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/2/4/4/244cb228379e4374552c69670533591aab8c3b3a.png)

Now that its active you can add panels to your menus

![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/b/9/c/b9c7e8cf845557a629948d5e3f213cdadf78bf53.png)

![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/7/0/1/701df1be73572dd1f8509515f349a0bbf140d362.png)

Using the interactive option is always my last ditch effort to see if I can add something to my menus!
