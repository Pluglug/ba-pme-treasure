I started my journey in 3D with Rhinoceros 3D. There was a great built-in ability to record macros and place them in menus, icons, etc. When switching to Blender, I immediately started looking for such an opportunity, but I did not find it. Again, it was suggested to write scripts in python and run them through the editor. I was already starting to think…, but then I found PME and fully switched to Blender. I see a lot of both free and paid addons (sometimes even at the price of PME) that offer functions that can be created in PME in literally 10 minutes. It is also quite common to combine scripts with Blender commands. At the moment, I just can’t imagine Blender without PME.
