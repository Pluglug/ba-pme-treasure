As Blender version 3.2 is soon getting officially released (it’s in RC as of writing this), I’m jumping in to mention that the click-drag mode for pie menus in PME mode no longer works in this version and newer (3.3 alpha).

Click-drag works for other pie menus in Blender, but not when assigning a click-drag hotkey action to menus through the PME interface.

As always, thanks for your work on the addon!

A bit further digging points to 3.2 api changes as being the problem:  
" Tweak events have been removed from `KeyMapItem.type`. (`EVT_TWEAK_L`, `EVT_TWEAK_M`, `EVT_TWEAK_R`)"

<https://wiki.blender.org/wiki/Reference/Release_Notes/3.2/Python_API#Breaking_Changes>
