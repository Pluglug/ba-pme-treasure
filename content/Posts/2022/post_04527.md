## 💬 Content

I’m super happy I bought this addon. I tried doing my version of Wazou RMB pie menu and I’m super happy with the result. It’s still a work in progress and I’m slowly migrating Wazou’s operators into my own addon. I’m learning a lot by experimenting with his code.

In case this is useful to someone: Blender’s Context Menus can coexist with RMB pie menus (or any other menu/operator/modal) by setting them to ‘click’ and ‘click drag’ respectively.

Also a little quirk. PME keymaps reset to ‘press’ instead of ‘click drag’ after I add a new pie menu or change the keymap in PME settings. I can see they appear as ‘click drag’ in PME settings but ‘press’ in Blender keymap section. I’m still trying to figure out this bug, If I don’t tinker with my menus they work fine and stay in their modes. I’m using Blender 3.2.2 and the latest version of the addon.

Overall PME just keeps on blowing my mind!
