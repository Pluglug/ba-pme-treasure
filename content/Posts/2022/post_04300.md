I see no need to worry about it.

I know the creators of the Photographer Add-On have talked about it a few times.  
Due to tax issues, it appears that creators are obligated to report their location to Blendermarket.

![](https://blenderartists.org/user_avatar/blenderartists.org/chafouin/48/629120_2.png) [Photographer - Camera Exposure, White Balance, Autofocus, Physical Lights, Bokeh, Render Queue](<https://blenderartists.org/t/photographer-camera-exposure-white-balance-autofocus-physical-lights-bokeh-render-queue/1101721/1165>) [Released Scripts and Themes](</c/coding/released-scripts-and-themes/50>)

> Some important information for BlenderMarket customers: Photographer 4 won’t be available on BlenderMarket, until they set up a VAT solution for European businesses. If you already bought it, you can still receive support and updates on BlenderMarket. If you are interested in buying the add-on, please use Gumroad: <https://chafouin.gumroad.com/l/HPrCY> Little tip: if you bought the add-on on Gumroad and need support, simply reply to the receipt email you received after the transaction. 

PME is not the only add-on that disappeared from blendermarket around the same time. Several products were missing from my blendermarket wishlist. And there were several add-ons that returned during the sale period.
