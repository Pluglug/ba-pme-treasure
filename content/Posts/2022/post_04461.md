It’s pretty simple.  
1: You press the little icon before the slot to edit it (or from the Menu on the other end of the row)  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/8/1/2/8124c09fd4ea2e151fe02a4b62d8773574346a31_2_690x139.png)image1528×308 45.7 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/8/1/2/8124c09fd4ea2e151fe02a4b62d8773574346a31.png> "image")

  
2: Then you go to the Menu tab, and select the macro by name, and click OK  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/8/4/d/84db143f7b702f0a6e3ce34643590d5ecf2d8710_2_690x107.png)image1751×273 24.3 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/8/4/d/84db143f7b702f0a6e3ce34643590d5ecf2d8710.png> "image")

Regarding mapping different actions to the same button press, that’s what Stack Keys are for.  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/a/b/b/abb8f2cf56f3d273a4fb28e98e7610d85eb202ef_2_690x122.png)image1621×287 44.6 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/a/b/b/abb8f2cf56f3d273a4fb28e98e7610d85eb202ef.png> "image")

  
Just set the commands you want to invoke with the button in the order you want to invoke them.
