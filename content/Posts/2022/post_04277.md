Open: Blender\3.1\scripts\addons\pie_menu_editor\bl_utils.py

Go to line 457 and change this:
    
    
            min_x = w2 + CC.POPUP_PADDING + offset
            max_x = context.window.width - (
                self.width >> 1) - CC.POPUP_PADDING - offset
    

For this:
    
    
            min_x = int(w2 + CC.POPUP_PADDING + offset)
            max_x = int(context.window.width - (
                self.width >> 1) - CC.POPUP_PADDING - offset)
    

It fixed it for me at least
