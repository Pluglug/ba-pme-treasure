This is awesome and does work much better! It is now useable, although there does still seem to be a bug unfortunately; perhaps some conditions have been overlooked:

  1. Create a simple stack key with it launching the asset browser in a popup window e.g. with a command bpy.ops.pme.popup_area(area=‘ASSETS’,width=1000, center=False, height=800)

  2. Save everything and restart Blender. Move the mouse cursor over the properties area and press the hotkey. This initially converts the properies area into the asset browser instead of launching the AB window.

  3. Open the preferences window (edit > preferences) and press the AB hotkey when the mouse is over it. This sets the preferences window to the AB.

This also happens with the 3D Viewport if it is maximised first (usually with Ctrl + Spacebar). Pressing the AB hotkey then takes over and changes the viewport to the AB.

Thanks a lot for looking into this.
