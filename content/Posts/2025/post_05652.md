## 💬 Content

**Release 1.19.1-beta.2 is now available.**  
Please help test it on Blender 5.0.

PME temporarily stores _all_ internal states — settings, menu structures, commands — and restores them when switching sessions.  
Blender 5.0 changed core behavior, and this restore logic no longer works reliably, which means the entire add-on is affected.

Since this whole concept is likely unnecessary in modern Blender, I simply disabled the restore feature. It works in my environment, but I need confirmation from yours as well.

**Checklist**

  * PME launches correctly on 5.0
  * Edit some PME settings and leave them unsaved
  * Switch to a new session (File > New) and check whether the entries persist
