## 💬 Content

PME stores its settings in Blender’s addon preferences. If you’ve reset Blender settings, that data may be lost.

However, PME uses `.json` files for backups and exports. Check this folder for autosave files:

`C:\Users\yourname\AppData\Roaming\Blender Foundation\Blender\4.x\scripts\addons\pie_menu_editor_data\backups`

You might still find your pie menus there.
