For those who are wondering about the same thing and want to display the data, you can do draw panel:
    
    
    panel("DATA_PT_shape_keys", frame=False, header=True, expand=None)
    

if you enable header, you will have an clickable/expandable title header (See Shape Keys).

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/c/8/9/c890d3145f3a071e631ca732b63f914f5f2bde5b_2_311x500.png)image462×742 19.3 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/c/8/9/c890d3145f3a071e631ca732b63f914f5f2bde5b.png> "image")

You can do it with other mesh properties (DATA_PT), as found here in properties_data_mesh.py

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/4/5/8/4582da32b876602db2f11b83d6bf85fd3d4e49e3_2_600x500.png)image1108×922 79.7 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/4/5/8/4582da32b876602db2f11b83d6bf85fd3d4e49e3.png> "image")

You can also do object PTs:  

[![image](https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/optimized/4X/d/9/a/d9abd651fee1ec3ac76993ecc542722c33002921_2_690x434.png)image868×547 50.3 KB](<https://blenderartists.org/uploads/default/original/4X/d/9/a/d9abd651fee1ec3ac76993ecc542722c33002921.png> "image")

It draws the panels as is, so it’s not exactly fully customizable like I wanted, but for now it will do.

For modifiers, I assumed DATA_PT_modifiers would work, but it doesn’t display the list, just the new modifier button. I’ll look further into it and reply to this when I figure it out.
