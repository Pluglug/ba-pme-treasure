By the way, `VIEW3D_PT_tools_brush_stroke_smooth_stroke` can’t fully utilize this mechanism…!  
Why? Because Blender hasn’t implemented it!

Compare `SmoothStrokePanel` and `DisplayPanel`.  
While DisplayPanel evaluates `is_popover` within its draw method and renders properties accordingly, `SmoothStrokePanel` doesn’t do this… This could be your chance to submit a PR to Blender!
