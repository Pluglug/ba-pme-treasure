That’s actually the expected behavior. PME tries to call the operator as it’s set up, so if Kushiro’s addon isn’t installed in your environment, a reference error will naturally occur.  
This kind of maintenance—removing or cleaning up old settings—needs to be handled by the user, not PME itself.  
If you’re experiencing any other issues, feel free to let me know.
