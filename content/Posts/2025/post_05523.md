## 💬 Content

In recent Blender versions, the line size between Areas changes continuously according to the Resolution Scale. This affects the size calculation of SideAreaToggle.

I will consider a fix method later, but as an immediate measure you can take, please set the Line Width to Thin and adjust the Resolution Scale.
