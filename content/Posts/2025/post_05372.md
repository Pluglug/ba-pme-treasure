There is a fix for certain panel menus added to a popup dialog

in `\pie_menu_editor\panel_utils.py`
    
    
    Line 794:     setattr(p, is_popover,False)
    Line 823:         if hasattr(p, "draw_header"):
                          setattr(p, "is_popover",True)
