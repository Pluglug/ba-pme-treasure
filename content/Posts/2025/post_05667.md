Thanks for the report.  
Please open a GitHub issue and fill out the Bug Report template completely.

[Issues · Pluglug/pie-menu-editor-fork](<https://github.com/Pluglug/pie-menu-editor-fork/issues>)

I can’t investigate this without full information, so make sure you include all of the following:

  * Your exact Blender version
  * Your PME-F version
  * A screen recording showing the delay
  * The System Console output captured during the slowdown  
(Top Bar → Window → Toggle System Console)
  * Your PME setup JSON export
  * Clear steps to reproduce the issue on your side

Without these details, it’s not possible to determine whether the problem comes from your configuration, your environment, or something in PME-F itself.

Once the complete issue is submitted, I’ll review it.
