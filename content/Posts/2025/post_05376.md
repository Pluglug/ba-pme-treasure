Yes, I have good news! I’ve been able to get in touch with roaoao. While he mentioned he doesn’t have much time for PME currently, his passion for the project is still there:

> _(My message: I believe PME deserves sustainable development and shines brightest with your guidance.)_
> 
> Yes, PME deserves sustainable development. I’m ashamed I don’t have enough time for this. But with your help and the help of the community we can achieve this.

About the version 1.18.8 you found - I’ve checked it and confirmed it’s roaoao’s own update, different from my PME-Fork 1.18.8. His version includes critical fixes implemented in a more elegant way than my approach, reminding me that PME is truly his creation. To avoid confusion, I’ll be removing my PME-Fork 1.18.8.

While his 1.18.8 is completely usable, there are still some minor improvements that could be made. I plan to continue working on these refinements for a potential 1.18.9 update, building upon his current version.

Thank you for your continued support and attention to PME’s development!
