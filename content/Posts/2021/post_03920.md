Since 2.93 if i want PME working correctly i have to remove its reimported folder from C:\Users[user name]\AppData\Roaming\Blender Foundation\Blender\3.0\scripts\addons at first. After that need to make clean installation ( like any other addon ), than import ( previously exported from earlier Blender version) PME settings.
