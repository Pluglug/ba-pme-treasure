## 💬 Content

As you’re likely aware, the `wm.pmi_edit` operator allows you to directly edit menu items in Pie Menu Editor. Assigning this function to a hotkey for quick access could increase the risk of accidentally damaging important menus (with no undo option).

Since posts here are visible to anyone, I prefer not to share executable code directly. Instead, I will send you the information in a private message, so please check your inbox.
