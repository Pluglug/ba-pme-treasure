I believe it’s not possible to achieve the styling you are looking for since the menu theme is determined by Blender’s settings. However, if you need some visual enhancements, I recommend enriching the custom icons. You know where the PME add-on folder is located, right? You can store and use images in the `icons` folder inside that folder. I have extracted image files from many add-ons I’ve purchased and placed them there (Note: Do not modify the default icons. I accidentally renamed one and crashed PME).

Furthermore, free icon sites for web development are recommended as sources for obtaining icons, such as [icons8.com](<http://icons8.com>). For example, on this site, you can customize the provided icons to your liking by changing their colors and other properties.
